VibeSpin Documentation Hub

Welcome to the VibeSpin documentation. VibeSpin is a high-performance Python framework for simulating and analyzing two-dimensional lattice spin models, including the foundational Ising, XY, and Clock models.

Note

Notebook Source of Truth: The notebook files located under docs/source/ are symlinks to the original files in the root notebooks/ directory. Edits must be made in notebooks/, not in docs/source/. Do not replace the symlinks with standalone copies.
